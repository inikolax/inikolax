### Hi, I'm Nikolay

I build **[Remiqora](https://github.com/inikolax/remiqora)**, a local AI music studio:
ACE-Step 1.5 and YuE2-3B in one interface, with stem separation, MIDI transcription,
LoRA training and a built-in multitrack editor. Runs on your own GPU (NVIDIA on Windows/Linux,
Apple Silicon on macOS), no cloud.
