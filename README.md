### Hi, I'm Nikolay

I build local AI studios that run on your own GPU, with no cloud and no account: **Remiqora** for music
and **Remiqora Video** for video. Both are open source, and both install only the models' official weights.

      <h3><a href="https://github.com/inikolax/remiqora">Remiqora</a></h3>
      A local AI music studio. ACE-Step 1.5, YuE2-3B and Stable Audio 3 in one interface: describe a song or a sound
      in your own words and a local language model writes the prompt; split the track into stems, add parts with
      the AI arranger and finish it in the built-in multitrack editor.

      <h3><a href="https://github.com/inikolax/remiqora-video">Remiqora Video</a></h3>
      A local AI video studio. A couple of sentences become a storyboard written by a local language model;
      MiniMax H3, LTX 2.5 / 2.3 or WAN 2.2 shoot it scene by scene with sound. Then montage with a DaVinci Resolve
      export, a node graph for versions, music videos cut to a song and a 1080p / 4K / HDR10 finish.
      <br><br>
      Windows · NVIDIA RTX, 16 GB
