### Hi, I'm Nikolay

I build **[Remiqora](https://github.com/inikolax/remiqora)**, a local AI music studio:
ACE-Step 1.5, YuE2-3B and Stable Audio 3 in one interface. Describe a song or a sound in your own
words and a local language model writes the prompt for the engine; then split the track into stems,
add parts with the AI arranger and finish it in the built-in multitrack editor. MIDI transcription
and LoRA training are there too. Runs on your own GPU (NVIDIA on Windows/Linux, Apple Silicon on macOS),
no cloud.
